Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tracking your device.

Your Ticket ID is located at the top-right corner of the physical receipt given to you at our center, or in the confirmation email you received.

Status levels include 'Received', 'Under Diagnostic', 'Awaiting Parts', 'Repairing', 'Quality Check', and 'Ready for Pickup'.

Our system is linked directly to our technicians' workstations. Updates happen the moment they move to the next stage of repair.

Once a repair has entered the 'Repairing' stage, online cancellation is not possible. Please call your service center directly for urgent requests.
