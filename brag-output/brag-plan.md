# Brag Plan: FixTrack

## What is this app?
FixTrack is a white-label repair tracking platform — like Shopify, but for gadget repair shops. Customers book repairs online and track their device step-by-step through six milestones. Staff manage everything from a built-in dashboard.

## The angle
This is a real, functional tool for a real industry. The video should feel like a polished product announcement: clean, professional, and let the product speak for itself. The strongest material is the status tracking flow — a customer enters their ticket ID and sees exactly where their device is in the pipeline. That's the centerpiece.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Bold text: "Track Your Device Repair In Real Time" — the product's own hero headline. Fast entry, immediately shows the search bar with ticket ID "1042" being typed in. The hook earns the next 18 seconds because it makes the viewer think: "oh, I can see exactly where my phone is."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The 6-step milestone tracker animating across: Device Received → Diagnostic → Awaiting Parts → Active Repair → Quality Check → Ready for Pickup. The "Active Repair" step is highlighted/active.
- Cost breakdown panel sliding in: line items with ₱ prices, subtotal, VAT, total.
- Dashboard ticket table — multiple rows of active repairs with status badges.

## Outro / punchline
"FixTrack" logo slam with tagline: "Fast, reliable device repairs you can count on."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: Customer types ticket "1042" into the search bar → Key action: Status page loads showing the 6-step milestone tracker with "Active Repair" highlighted, device image (iPhone 13), assigned technician → Result: Cost breakdown appears, total amount in bold.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: clean SaaS product demo for a repair shop platform
- Interpretation: Feature-forward, professional. Clean reveals, card-style layouts. No jokes — this is an earnest product for real shops. Let the UI speak.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook & Search — 4s
The FixTrack dark-mode UI fades in. Hero headline "Track Your Device Repair In Real Time" is already visible. A search bar is center-screen. A cursor appears and types "1042" into the search field, then clicks "Track Repair". The scene transitions to the status view.
Sequential/interaction: yes — typing animation character by character "1042", then a simulated click on the Track Repair button
Audio intent: professional and inviting. Keyboard ticks establish the product as interactive.
Audio-coupled idea: keypress sounds per character, then a click sound on the button
Music: warm upbeat bed, fading in
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Status Tracker — 6s
The status page appears. The 6-step horizontal milestone tracker animates in: "Device Received" → "Diagnostic" → "Awaiting Parts" → "Active Repair" (highlighted with accent color) → "Quality Check" → "Ready for Pickup" (dimmed/upcoming). Below the tracker, the device info panel shows: iPhone 13, serial G0H29KJML01, with a device image. The "Active Repair" badge pulses or glows.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the 6 milestone steps appear one by one left to right, each with a subtle pop. The "Active Repair" step gets a highlight treatment. Device info card slides in below.
Audio intent: building confidence. Each step landing gives a sense of progress and reliability.
Audio-coupled idea: 6 milestone steps arrive sequentially with soft interface sounds on every other beat. Active Repair step gets a slightly louder accent.
Music: bed continues, milestone reveals align to beat grid
Transition mood: smooth → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Cost Breakdown & Dashboard — 6s
Split or sequential moment. First half: the cost breakdown card slides in from the right — line items appear (Replacement OLED Screen ₱11,700 / Adhesive Seal ₱1,000 / Labor ₱2,800), subtotal, VAT 12%, and bold total ₱17,360. Second half: cut to the staff dashboard showing the ticket table with multiple rows, status badges in different colors, "Active Repairs: 4" and "Ready for Pickup: 1" stat cards at top.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cost line items appear one by one. Dashboard ticket rows populate.
Audio intent: professional payoff. The cost breakdown feels transparent and the dashboard feels powerful.
Audio-coupled idea: cost items drop in with subtle card sounds. Dashboard stat cards pop with light interface sounds.
Music: bed continues at full energy
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Logo & Tagline — 4s
Clean dark background. The wrench icon (from the favicon) scales in, then "FixTrack" in Space Grotesk bold appears. Below it, the tagline: "Fast, reliable device repairs you can count on." Hold for reading. Music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: yes — wrench icon first, then product name, then tagline
Audio intent: confident landing. One clear announcement cue.
Audio-coupled idea: wrench icon appearance gets a bell/announcement sound. Music gently fades out.
Music: fading out over 2s
Transition mood: end
